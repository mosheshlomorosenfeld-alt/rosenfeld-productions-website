03 / BIO

MEET
MOSHE.

Moshe Rosenfeld is a singer, songwriter and music producer building modern music with a strong sense of story, energy and identity. His work moves between pop, EDM, cinematic production and Jewish music — always with the goal of making something people actually want to play again.

As the founder of Rosenfeld Productions, Moshe works across the entire creative process: writing, arranging, recording, producing and preparing music for release or live performance.
